Help test Sentrybell

A loud lock-screen alarm for Tapo and Kasa camera alerts, for Android.

Sentrybell is in a closed test on Google Play. Google requires every new app to be tried by real people for 14 days before it can be released, and that is where you come in. It is free, and joining takes about two minutes.

How to join

  1. Join the tester group with the Google account you use on your Android phone.
    Join the tester group
  2. Open the test link (your Google Play opt-in page) and tap Become a tester.
    Become a tester on Google Play
    Use the same Google account you joined the group with.
  3. Install Sentrybell from Google Play using the link on that page.
  4. Keep it installed and use it for 14 days. Please do not uninstall it early: the test only counts if testers stay opted in the whole time.

What to try

  • Open the menu, choose Test alarm, then Ring. You do not need a camera for this. Lock your phone while it rings and press STOP from the lock screen.
  • Open the menu, then Setup & permissions, and see how far it gets you.
  • If you own a Tapo or Kasa camera, add it with the + button, typing its name as it appears in its notifications, and see whether a real alert rings. This is the most useful thing you can tell us, especially for Kasa, which has not yet been tried with a live camera.
  • Look for anything confusing, slow or broken, in English or Spanish.
  • Email us which camera brand you own (Tapo, Kasa, or another) at contact@tgalphalabs.com, even if you do not test a live alert. It tells us which cameras to support next.

Tell us what you find at contact@tgalphalabs.com. Honest feedback, good or bad, is what helps.

What you need to know first

  • You need an Android phone running Android 8.0 or newer, and a Google account.
  • To work, Sentrybell asks for notification access. It reads only the Tapo and Kasa apps' notifications, keeps everything on your phone and sends nothing anywhere. Read the privacy policy for the details.
  • Joining the group shares your Google account's email address with the app's developer only. Other testers cannot see it.
  • There is no payment, and we do not pay for ratings or reviews, so please only leave one if you mean it.
