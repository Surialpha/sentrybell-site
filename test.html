Help test Sentrybell

A loud lock-screen alarm for Tapo and Kasa camera alerts, for Android.

Sentrybell is in a closed test on Google Play: a private test, open only to people who sign up, before the public release. Google asks new developers to have real people try their app for 14 days before it can be released, and that is where you come in. It is free, you do not need a camera, and joining takes about five minutes, including a short wait in the middle.

Before you start

  • Do this on your Android phone, running Android 8.0 or newer (you can check in Settings, then About phone).
  • Pick ONE Google account and use it in steps 1, 2 and 3. A personal Gmail is the safest choice, because work or school accounts may not be able to join tests. To see your accounts, open the Play Store app and tap your picture or initial at the top right. If Google ever asks you to "Choose an account", pick that same one. Most problems come from using a different account in a step.
  • Joining the group shares your Google account's email address with us, the developer, and nobody else.

How to join

  1. Join the tester group This is a Google Group, our list of testers. Tap the button, then tap Join group and confirm if it asks. Nothing else to do there. Use your chosen account.
    Join the tester group

    Now wait about 5 minutes before step 2 (a timer helps). Google needs a little time to notice that you joined.

  2. Become a tester Same account. Tap the button, then tap Become a tester on the page that opens. The page should then say you are a tester.
    Become a tester on Google Play
  3. Install Sentrybell Same account. Tap the button to open Sentrybell in Google Play, then tap Install.
    Download Sentrybell on Google Play
    During the test, searching for "Sentrybell" in Google Play may not find it, so use this button. If the page says Item not found, see "Something not working?" below.
  4. Keep it for 14 days Keep Sentrybell installed and open it now and then. Please do not uninstall it, do not tap Leave the program on the Google Play tester page, and do not leave the Google Group. Google counts the test only if at least 12 testers stay signed up the whole time. Then try the things in "After you install" below.

Something not working?

The page says "Item not found" or the app is not available

This is usually one of two things.

  • A different Google account. The account that joined the group must be the one signed in to the Play Store. To see which account joined, open the group page from step 1: your account is shown at the top right. To see or switch the Play Store account, tap your picture or initial at the top right of the Play Store app. If they differ, switch the Play Store to the account that joined, or repeat steps 1 and 2 with the Play Store's account.
  • Too soon. Google can take a while to notice steps 1 and 2: usually a few minutes, sometimes up to several hours. Wait, then tap the step 3 button again.

Also check that you did steps 1 and 2 in that order. If it still fails after a few hours, email us (see below).

I do not see the "Become a tester" button

You are probably not signed in to Google in your phone's browser, or you are signed in with an account that has not joined the group. Sign in with the right account (top right of the page) and open step 2 again. If a page asks you to "Choose an account", pick the one you chose at the start.

I started on a computer

Please do all the steps on your phone: it is the simplest way, and the app installs on the phone anyway. Open this page again on your phone and go through steps 2 and 3 there.

It still does not work

Email contact@tgalphalabs.com and tell us which step you are on and what the screen says. A screenshot helps.

After you install: what to try

  • Open Sentrybell, tap the three dots at the top right, choose Test alarm, then Ring. It will be loud, and you do not need a camera for this. Lock your phone while it rings and press STOP from the lock screen.
  • In the same three-dots menu, choose Setup & permissions and see how far it gets you.
  • If you own a Tapo or Kasa camera, add it with the + button, typing its name as it appears in its notifications, and see whether a real alert rings. This is the most useful thing you can tell us, especially for Kasa, which has not yet been tried with a live camera.
  • Look for anything confusing, slow or broken, in English or Spanish.
  • Email us which camera brand you own (Tapo, Kasa, or another) at contact@tgalphalabs.com, even if you do not test a live alert. It tells us which cameras to support next.

Tell us what you find at contact@tgalphalabs.com. Honest feedback, good or bad, is what helps.

What you need to know

  • To work, Sentrybell asks for notification access, a permission Android asks you to allow. It reads only the Tapo and Kasa apps' notifications, keeps everything on your phone and sends nothing anywhere. Read the privacy policy for the details.
  • There is no payment, and we never pay for ratings or reviews. During the test, Google Play does not take public reviews, so please send feedback by email.
