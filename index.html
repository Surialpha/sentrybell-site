Sentrybell

A loud, lock-screen alarm for your Tapo and Kasa camera alerts.

An Android app by T&G Alphalabs. Coming soon to Google Play.

Want to help? Join the closed test and try Sentrybell before it is released.

What it does

Camera apps send a quiet notification when someone is at the door. Sentrybell watches for those notifications and, for the cameras and events you choose, rings a loud alarm with sound, vibration and flashlight, and opens a full-screen alert over your lock screen. You press STOP without unlocking.

  • Choose per camera which events ring: person, motion, pet or tamper.
  • Set the hours each camera is active, overnight included, plus sound, vibration, flashlight and volume.
  • A history that groups alerts into incidents and shows what rang and what stayed quiet, and why.
  • A Setup screen that checks everything the alarm needs and takes you to the right Android setting.

Works with

The Tapo app and the Kasa Smart app. Sentrybell reads the notifications those apps already show. It does not connect to your cameras and cannot see any video.

Free and Pro

Free: one camera with the full alarm, schedules and history. Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play, not a subscription, and adds unlimited cameras and your own alarm tone for each camera. The alarm itself is never limited.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics. Your notifications, camera names and history stay on your phone. Read the privacy policy.
